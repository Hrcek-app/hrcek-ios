# Saving pages

[Slovenščina](../sl/saving-pages.md) · [Contents](index.md)

You save pages from your browser. [Sign in](sign-in.md) in the app
first; after that you rarely need to open it.

## From Safari

1. Open the page you want to keep.
2. Open the page menu (the button left of the address) and tap
   **Share**. On some versions of iOS the Share button is directly in
   the toolbar.
3. Tap **Hrček** in the row of apps.

The Hrček sheet opens with the page's address, its title when the
browser provides one, and room for notes and tags. Change what you like,
then tap **Save**. The sheet says **Saved** and closes by itself after a
moment. **Cancel** closes it without saving anything.

### Picture

When the page has pictures, the sheet offers one to save with the
entry: the one the page names as its own, or else its largest. Switch
**Include picture** off to save the entry without one. Tap **Change**
to see every picture the page offers, and tap the one you want; tap the
large picture, or go back, when you have chosen.

Shared from Safari, the sheet offers every picture on the page,
including those further down that have not loaded yet. Other browsers
and apps pass on only the address, so the sheet offers just the
pictures a page names for its link previews.

For a page you saved before, the sheet starts with the picture the
entry already has, marked **current** in Change. Switching Include
picture off removes it when you save. If the entry has no picture,
Include picture starts off, so fixing a typo does not add one.

Sometimes the sheet cannot show the picture an entry already has and
says so instead. Saving leaves it as it is.

If the picture cannot be saved, the entry is saved anyway and the
sheet says why the picture was not. It then stays open until you tap
**Done**.

### Tags

Type a tag and tap the return key, or type a comma, to add it. Tap a
tag to remove it. Capitals do not make a different tag: adding
`Watches` when `watches` is already there does nothing. A tag you typed
but did not add yet is added when you tap Save.

As you type, the sheet offers the tags you already use that begin with
what you typed. Tap one to add it. Tags the page already has are not
offered.

### Your fields

The fields you defined for your entries on the website — Priority, to
begin with — are folded away under **Your fields**. Tap it to open
them. A field you leave empty is saved empty.

If a choice field holds a value it no longer offers, the sheet shows it
marked **(no longer offered)** and saving keeps it. If the sheet cannot
read your fields, it says so and shows only the ones the entry already
has; saving then leaves the others as they are.

Other browsers work the same way: find their Share button, then choose
Hrček.

## If Hrček is not in the row

Scroll the row of apps to the end and tap **More**. Find Hrček, switch
it on, and drag it towards the front of the list so it is easy to
reach.

## A page you have already saved

If you share a page that is already in your Hrček, the sheet says
**Already saved** and shows the entry as it is: its title, notes and
tags. Change what you like and tap **Update**; the sheet says
**Updated**. Whatever you leave alone stays as it was.

## When saving fails

The sheet stays open, keeps what you typed and says what went wrong
under the form. Tap **Save** again once the problem is fixed. Nothing
is saved until it succeeds. See [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

When Hrček can't be reached, the sheet keeps the page on the phone
instead and sends it later. See
[When Hrček can't be reached](offline.md).
