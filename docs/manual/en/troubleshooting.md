# Troubleshooting

[Slovenščina](../sl/troubleshooting.md) · [Contents](index.md)

**Hrček is not in the Share sheet.** Scroll the row of apps to the end,
tap **More**, and switch Hrček on. If it is not in that list either,
open the Hrček app once, then try again.

**"Open the Hrček app and sign in first."** The Share sheet does not
know you yet. Open the app and [sign in](sign-in.md), then share the
page again.

**"Hrček no longer accepts this phone's sign-in."** The access token
for this phone was removed on the website, or it expired. Open the app
and [sign in](sign-in.md) again, then share the page again.

**"The entry was saved, but the picture could not be attached."** The
rest of the message says why: often the picture is in a format Hrček
cannot read, or too large. Share the page again and choose another
picture under **Change**, or switch **Include picture** off.

**"Hrček can't be reached."** The phone is offline, or your Hrček is
down. The Share sheet keeps the page on the phone and sends it later;
see [When Hrček can't be reached](offline.md).

**"You're offline."** When signing in, connect to the internet and try
again. The Share sheet does not say this: it keeps the page for later
instead.

**"Can't reach the server."** When signing in: the server may be down,
or the address may be wrong. Open your Hrček in the browser to check.
If the address changed, sign out in the app and sign in again with the
new one.

**"Your sign-in has expired or was revoked."** The access token for
this phone was removed on the website, or it expired. Sign in again.

**"The server's answer wasn't understood."** The address probably
points at a website that is not a Hrček. Check it in the app.

**"This page is already saved. Check what it holds, then save
again."** Hrček could not check the page when the sheet opened, so it
checked again before saving and found the page already saved. The form
now shows what is saved; make your changes again and tap **Update**.

**Something else.** Tap **Save** again. If it keeps failing, note what
the message says and ask whoever runs your Hrček.
