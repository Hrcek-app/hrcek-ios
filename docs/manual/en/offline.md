# When Hrček can't be reached

[Slovenščina](../sl/offline.md) · [Contents](index.md)

Sometimes the Share sheet cannot reach your Hrček: the phone is offline,
or the server is down for a while. You can still save the page. The
phone keeps it and sends it to Hrček later.

## Saving for later

When the sheet opens and cannot reach Hrček, it says so at the top:
"Hrček can't be reached. Saving keeps this entry on this phone until it
can." Fill in the title, notes, tags and picture as usual, then tap
**Save for later**. The sheet says **Kept for later** and closes by
itself.

The same happens when Hrček answered as the sheet opened but cannot be
reached by the time you tap **Save**: the sheet keeps the entry instead
of losing what you typed.

Your fields are not shown while Hrček can't be reached, because they
could not be loaded. A kept page leaves them as they are.

## Where kept pages go

Kept pages stay on this phone, in Hrček's own storage, until they reach
Hrček. They are sent the next time you open the app, or the next time
you save a page while Hrček can be reached: after **Saved**, the sheet
says "Sending 1 waiting page…" and then what happened, such as "1 entry
saved."

A kept page is removed from the phone as soon as it is saved to Hrček,
or when you delete it.

## The list of waiting pages

While pages wait, the app opens on them: **Waiting to sync** lists each
page with its title, site, when you kept it and its tags. Opening the
app also sends them, so often the list is already empty by the time you
look. A line says what the last sync did, such as "1 entry saved." or
"Hrček can't be reached.": under the first button, or under "Nothing
is waiting." once the list is empty.

- **Sync now** sends what waits once more, for example when Hrček is
  back. It is greyed out while nothing can be sent.
- **Sign in to sync these.** shows instead when you are signed out. Tap
  it to sign in; the pages are sent once you are.
- To delete a page, swipe it to the left and tap **Delete**. The app
  asks first, because a page that has not reached Hrček is gone for good.
- A page Hrček already has says "Hrček already has this page." It is
  not sent over Hrček's entry; tap it to choose which to keep (see
  below).
- A page Hrček refused shows Hrček's reason in red. Delete it, or share
  the page again to fix what the reason says.
- A page kept while you were signed in to another server says "Saved
  for …, which is not the server you're signed in to." It is only ever
  sent to that server, so sign in there to send it, or delete it.

The gear at the top left opens the usual screen: signing in or out, how
to save a page, and the language. While pages wait, it also says how
many, such as "2 pages waiting to sync". **Done** closes it.

When nothing is left, the list says "Nothing is waiting." and what the
sync did, including any page saved without its picture. Tap **Done**
and the app shows its usual screen until pages wait again. If you leave
the app instead, it opens on its usual screen next time.

## Pages Hrček already has

Someone may save the same page to Hrček, from another phone or the
website, while yours waits. Yours is then not sent over theirs: the row
says "Hrček already has this page." Tap it to see both side by side,
**Yours** above and **On Hrček** below, each with its title, notes,
tags, fields and picture.

- **Keep what's on Hrček** deletes your copy from the phone and leaves
  Hrček's entry as it is. It can only be tapped once Hrček's entry is
  shown, so you never throw yours away unseen.
- **Replace with mine** saves your copy over Hrček's entry, picture
  included, and removes it from the phone.
- If Hrček no longer has the page, the comparison says "Hrček no longer
  has this page." and offers **Save mine** instead.

While Hrček can't be reached, both buttons are greyed out and the
reason is shown in red; your copy stays as it is. Go back and open it
again later. If Hrček refuses your copy, it goes back to the list with
Hrček's reason.

A page Hrček has can only be compared while you are signed in to the
server it was kept for.

## Sharing a kept page again

Share a page that is waiting and the sheet opens on what you kept, not
on the page afresh, and says "Waiting to be saved to Hrček." Change
what you like. If Hrček can be reached, **Save** sends it now; if not,
**Save for later** keeps your changes in its place. One page is kept
once, never twice.

If Hrček refused a kept page, the sheet shows Hrček's reason under the
form. Fix what it says and save again.

If someone saved the same page to Hrček while yours was waiting, yours
is not sent over theirs, even if the phone has not tried to send it yet.
Sharing the page then shows Hrček's entry instead of your copy and says
"You also have an unsynced copy of this page. Saving here replaces it."
If Hrček can't be reached at that moment, the sheet shows your copy
instead, as a page that is waiting; saving it for later keeps it, and it
is checked against Hrček's entry again before it is sent.

## At most 100

The phone keeps at most 100 pages. With 100 waiting, the sheet says
"100 entries are already waiting to be saved. Sync or delete some
first." and keeps the form open, so nothing you typed is lost.
