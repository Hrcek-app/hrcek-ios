# When Hrček can't be reached

[Slovenščina](../sl/offline.md) · [Contents](index.md)

Sometimes the Share sheet cannot reach your Hrček: the phone is offline,
or the server is down for a while. You can still save the page. The
phone keeps it and sends it to Hrček later.

## Saving for later

When the sheet opens and cannot reach Hrček, it says so at the top:
"Hrček can't be reached. Saving keeps this entry on this phone until it
can." Fill in the title, notes, tags and picture as usual, then tap
**Save for later**. The sheet says **Kept for later** and closes by
itself.

The same happens when Hrček answered as the sheet opened but cannot be
reached by the time you tap **Save**: the sheet keeps the entry instead
of losing what you typed.

Your fields are not shown while Hrček can't be reached, because they
could not be loaded. A kept page leaves them as they are.

## Where kept pages go

Kept pages stay on this phone, in Hrček's own storage, until they reach
Hrček. They are sent the next time you save a page while Hrček can be
reached: after **Saved**, the sheet says "Sending 1 waiting page…" and
then what happened, such as "1 entry saved."

A kept page is removed from the phone as soon as it is saved to Hrček,
or when you delete it.

## Sharing a kept page again

Share a page that is waiting and the sheet opens on what you kept, not
on the page afresh, and says "Waiting to be saved to Hrček." Change
what you like. If Hrček can be reached, **Save** sends it now; if not,
**Save for later** keeps your changes in its place. One page is kept
once, never twice.

If Hrček refused a kept page, the sheet shows Hrček's reason under the
form. Fix what it says and save again.

If someone saved the same page to Hrček while yours was waiting, yours
is not sent over theirs, even if the phone has not tried to send it yet.
Sharing the page then shows Hrček's entry instead of your copy and says
"You also have an unsynced copy of this page. Saving here replaces it."
If Hrček can't be reached at that moment, the sheet shows your copy
instead, as a page that is waiting; saving it for later keeps it, and it
is checked against Hrček's entry again before it is sent.

## At most 100

The phone keeps at most 100 pages. With 100 waiting, the sheet says
"100 entries are already waiting to be saved. Sync or delete some
first." and keeps the form open, so nothing you typed is lost.
