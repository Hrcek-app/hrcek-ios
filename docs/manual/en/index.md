# Hrček for iOS

[Slovenščina](../sl/index.md)

Hrček for iOS saves the web page you are reading to your Hrček, the
place where your family keeps references to interesting things found
online.

You will not use the app much on its own. You save pages from your
browser: open the page, tap **Share**, and choose **Hrček**.

This is an early version. Signing in and saving pages arrive in the
next versions; this manual grows with them.
